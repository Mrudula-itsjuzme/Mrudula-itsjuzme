### currently in the lab

I’m an AI Engineering student at **Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham**. Most of my work sits in the overlap between research and software: computer vision, signal processing, machine learning, and systems where the interesting part starts after the first model works.

Lately that means **markerless motion capture**, gait kinematics, deep-learning experiments, evaluation pipelines, and figuring out how to make model outputs easier to inspect instead of simply trust.

I like keeping the messy middle visible: benchmarks, failed assumptions, edge cases, redesigns, and the version before the polished demo.
